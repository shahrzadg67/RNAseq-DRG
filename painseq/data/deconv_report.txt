=====================================================================
 Naive-only vs injury-state deconvolution reference
=====================================================================

Reference composition:
  naive_fixed  19 cell types  (missing: Repair fibroblast)
  injury       20 cell types

  `Repair fibroblast` exists in only 6 naive cells across the whole atlas, below the
  20-cell minimum, so a naive-only reference cannot represent it at all. That was the
  motivation for building an injury-state reference.

--- MOUSE bulk: repair populations by condition (% of bulk) ---
  naive_fixed  Repair schwann_N   NP=0.395  Sham=0.314  SNI=1.558
  injury       Repair schwann_N   NP=0.000  Sham=0.000  SNI=0.000
  injury       Repair fibroblast  NP=0.000  Sham=0.000  SNI=0.000

=> The injury-state reference reports 0.000% repair cells in EVERY mouse sample,
   including the two SNI samples. The naive reference does not: it puts
   `Repair schwann_N` at ~1.3-1.8% in the SNI samples against 0.13-0.62% in Sham
   and MINP — roughly a 3-4x elevation, in the one condition where axotomy actually
   happened. So the naive reference gives the biologically sensible answer here and
   the injury reference does not.

   Why: MuSiC uses weighted NNLS. Signatures built from injured cells are far more
   extreme, and with 20 competing cell types the solver drives the rare repair
   components to exactly zero while other types absorb the signal (NF3 rises from
   2.09% to 6.68%, PEP1 collapses from 2.88% to 0.03%).

   CONCLUSION: do NOT switch the app's Deconvolution tab to an injury-state reference.
   The naive-only reference stays the better choice. The real caveat to record is that
   absolute proportions are sensitive to reference construction, so they should be read
   as relative comparisons across samples, never as absolute tissue composition.

--- Cross-species check on the injury reference ---
  rat     Repair schwann_N: mean 0.64%, max 5.50% | Repair fibroblast: mean 1.28%, max 4.32%
  monkey  Repair schwann_N: mean 8.09%, max 14.63% | Repair fibroblast: mean 0.00%, max 0.00%
  In macaque, `Repair schwann_N` is highest in the injured (ipsi) samples — mean ~10.8%
  versus ~7.3% in controls — which is the right direction, but the control samples
  already sit high, and rat shows naive samples as high as injured ones. Cross-species
  ortholog mapping adds enough noise that these numbers should not be over-read.
